Who We Are

A one-stop-shop for Iraq's industrial & energy growth

KINDA for Logistics and Oil Services is a premier multi-sector Iraqi company dedicated to delivering integrated operational solutions. We combine technical expertise with local market knowledge to support Iraq's industrial and energy growth — standing as a beacon of reliability in the logistics and oil sectors.

We specialize in comprehensive oilfield services, general contracting, and industrial supply, strategically positioned to serve major international oil companies (IOCs) and national entities with international standards and local expertise.

  • ✔ Rigorous adherence to international HSE standards
