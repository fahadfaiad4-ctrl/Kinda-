Who We Are

A premier multi-sector Iraqi company

KINDA for Logistics and Oil Services is a premier multi-sector Iraqi company dedicated to delivering integrated operational solutions. We combine technical expertise with local market knowledge to support Iraq's industrial and energy growth. KINDA stands as a beacon of reliability in the logistics and oil sectors — we pride ourselves on a "one-stop-shop" approach, ensuring that complex supply chains and technical projects are executed with precision, safety, and efficiency.

KINDA is a premier Iraqi-based company specializing in comprehensive oilfield services, general contracting, and industrial supply. Established with a vision to support Iraq's energy and infrastructure sectors, we combine local expertise with international standards to deliver high-quality solutions. We are strategically positioned to serve major international oil companies (IOCs) and national entities.

Our Mission
